Fona plays internet radio from the menu bar. When the news starts on Deutschlandfunk, Fona fades over to it and back to your station afterwards – like the traffic-announcement button of an old car radio.

## Getting started
- Click the Fona icon in the menu bar (a disc with an F).
- Under **All Stations** you search for a station (from two letters on). A click plays it, the star adds it to your **Favourites**.
- The big button at the top pauses and resumes.

## Favourites and Recently Played
- Drag favourites to sort them. Right-click a station to remove it from the favourites, rename it or remove it. You can also rename the playing station by right-clicking its name at the top.
- Volume: the slim bar at the bottom right, left of the power button. Click or drag, turn the mouse wheel with the pointer over it, or use the arrow keys. Clicking the small speaker mutes.
- Play/Pause: the play/pause key on the keyboard, the button on headphones and Control Center start and stop Fona as well (while Fona was the last app playing).
- **Recently Played** shows the last ten stations that are not favourites. The star next to one makes it a favourite.

## All Stations
- The list comes from the free directory radio-browser.info.
- The country menu chooses which countries are shown – or **Worldwide**.
- Fona deliberately shows no station logos: pictures from the internet are a way in for attackers. You see the initials instead.

## Add your own link
- The **+** next to the search opens “Add Station”. Paste the stream link and click **Add**.
- Fona checks the link first: you can only save it when it really delivers sound in a supported format (MP3, AAC or HLS).
- Web pages, files, stations on your home network or on this Mac, and encrypted streams are refused. The message says why.

## News switch
- Turn it on or off with the **News Switch** at the top of the window.
- Fona listens to Deutschlandfunk silently and recognises the start of the news by the **time signal** (the beeps before the news) and by the programme title. Playback starts with the first beep – not a word is missed.
- If you start Fona while the news is already on, it jumps straight in (live, from where the bulletin is).
- Afterwards your station continues live. **Back Now** takes you straight back to your station. While the bulletin is still running, a strip at the top offers **To the News** – it takes you back in, live.
- If you listen to Deutschlandfunk yourself or have paused, Fona switches nothing.
- **Settings → Schedule:** Fona only listens around the full hour (or full and half hour). Deutschlandfunk has news hourly at night and also at half past during the day.
- **Maximum length:** if for once Fona cannot tell the end from the programme title, it returns after this time (bulletins last 5–10 minutes).
- Data use: because Deutschlandfunk runs alongside, Fona uses about twice the data of one station – roughly 115 MB per hour. Without the news switch about half.

## Settings
At the bottom of the window: gear = **Settings**, open book = **Manual**, the power button on the right = **Quit Fona**. In Settings: news, countries of the station list, open at login, updates, language, self-test and problem reports.

## Security
- Fona runs in the macOS sandbox: it may use the internet, but not your files.
- The audio is decoded in a separate process without any permissions.
- Fona only connects to addresses on the internet – never to devices on your home network.
- Fona does not work behind mandatory proxies (some company networks) or with VPN tools that hand out fake addresses.

## Help and problem reports
- **Self-Test** checks every function with its own test data and sends an anonymous report.
- **Report a problem …** sends your description; e-mail, system info and log are optional.
- Reports never contain station names, links or titles. If sending fails, the report is saved in Fona’s “Reports” folder.
- **Show Log** reveals the log file in the Finder.

## Updates
Fona checks for updates automatically (at start and every 6 hours) and shows a note at the bottom of the window; one click installs. **Skip** hides that version until a newer one appears or you check yourself in Settings. Every update is signed; a modified or older one is never installed. Keep Fona in the Applications folder – from the Downloads folder it cannot replace itself.
